# Public Naming and Institutional-Status Rule

## Recommended public title

**Sahel Climate Actions — A Great Green Wall Multi-Actor Serious Game Prototype**

This title is preferable for public indexing because it gives the project a distinctive identity while avoiding a title structure that could be read as an official institutional product.

## Internal development name

The existing development identity may continue internally as:

`FAO / GREAT GREEN WALL / SAHEL`

## Required status language

Use at least one clear statement on every public landing page or repository README:

> This is an independently developed serious-game prototype for research, design, portfolio and human-playtest purposes. Institutional names and policy contexts are represented as subject matter and gameplay roles. No official commission or endorsement is implied.

## Avoid in public metadata unless later authorized

- “FAO official game”;
- “FAO serious game” as an unqualified product title;
- “developed for FAO”;
- “commissioned by FAO”;
- institutional logos used as endorsement signals;
- claims of validated policy effectiveness;
- claims that playtest values are predictive or operational data.

## Safe descriptive wording

- “models FAO as one of five institutional gameplay roles”;
- “Great Green Wall / Sahel policy context”;
- “independent serious-game prototype”;
- “Human Playtest Candidate”;
- “research and educational simulation”.

## Release-status vocabulary

Current:

`TTS HUMAN PLAYTEST CANDIDATE v2.5`

After complete tutorial validation:

`GUIDED TURN MANAGER v1.0`

After empirical playtesting and resulting corrections:

use a later numbered playtest or public release rather than retroactively describing v2.5 as final.
